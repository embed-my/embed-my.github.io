This is the player origin of Embed My

The pages here are the ones an Embed My snippet points at: the frame that plays an H5P package, the script that sizes it, and the sample packages. There is nothing to do on this address itself.

  • To make an embed, go to embed-my.org.
  • The frame is /h5p?src=<url of a .h5p file>; the sizing script is /h5p-resizer.js.
  • The player is h5p-offline-player (MIT). The H5P runtime it loads is GPL-3.0; its licence and notices are served here with it.
  • Source: github.com/embed-my/embed-my.github.io.
